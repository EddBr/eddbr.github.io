Beliefs as distributions

So far each arm had one number, its estimate $Q(a)$. A Bayesian agent keeps a whole curve instead: how plausible each possible value is. The curve's width says how sure the agent is, and that is what exploration needs.

The maths

A belief about $\theta \in [0, 1]$ is a probability density over it. The Beta distribution with parameters $\alpha, \beta > 0$ is

$$p(\theta) = \frac{\theta^{\alpha-1}(1-\theta)^{\beta-1}}{B(\alpha, \beta)}$$

$B(\alpha, \beta)$ is the constant that makes the area under the curve equal 1.

After $h$ heads and $t$ tails, the probability of that sequence given $\theta$ (the likelihood) is $\theta^h(1-\theta)^t$. Bayes' rule multiplies prior by likelihood:

$$\begin{aligned} p(\theta \mid \text{data}) &\propto p(\text{data} \mid \theta)\, p(\theta) \\ &\propto \theta^{h}(1-\theta)^{t} \\ &\qquad \cdot\, \theta^{\alpha-1}(1-\theta)^{\beta-1} \\ &= \theta^{\alpha+h-1}(1-\theta)^{\beta+t-1} \end{aligned}$$

That is the shape of another Beta. So the belief stays a Beta, and updating means adding counts:

$$\text{Beta}(\alpha, \beta) \;\xrightarrow{\;h \text{ heads},\ t \text{ tails}\;}\; \text{Beta}(\alpha + h,\ \beta + t)$$

A head adds 1 to $\alpha$, a tail adds 1 to $\beta$. The prior acts like $\alpha - 1$ heads and $\beta - 1$ tails seen before you start. With $\alpha = \beta = 1$ the belief is $\text{Beta}(1 + \text{heads},\ 1 + \text{tails})$.

The mean and variance of $\text{Beta}(\alpha, \beta)$ are

$$\mathbb{E}[\theta] = \frac{\alpha}{\alpha + \beta}$$ $$\operatorname{Var}[\theta] = \frac{\alpha\beta}{(\alpha+\beta)^2(\alpha+\beta+1)}$$

The mean is also the probability the next flip is heads. The variance shrinks roughly like $1/n$ with $n$ flips, so the width (standard deviation) shrinks like $1/\sqrt{n}$, the same rate as the UCB bonus in Lesson 9.

Two independent beliefs answer "which is better?": $P(\theta_A > \theta_B) = \int_0^1 p_A(x)\,P(\theta_B < x)\,dx$, the chance that A's value is $x$ and B's is below it, summed over $x$. Lesson 12 turns this into a way to choose arms.
