I receive messages asking about the Silicon Valley Fellowship. I want to give each email it's due effort, but I can be busy. I'm writing this blog to give all the information I can.
# What was the application process like?
My application was super quick. I filled out the form and then had a 10/15 minute interview.
# What are they looking for?
SVF filters for people who are ambitious, hungry and helpful.
I cannot remember the exact questions (and it's more fun to find out on the day!). These are some questions that are good to have an answer for:

 <ol>
  <li>Why do you want to do SVF?</li>
  <li>Why are you the right candidate?</li>
  <li>What makes you stand out?</li>
</ol> 

Don't worry about the scale of what you're working on. The SVF team want people who are starting small and trying super hard because these people would get the most out of the fellowship.
# Reaching out to people
It's a good idea to reach out to people to see what SVF *can* be. However, keep in mind each alumnus will share *their* experience.
People have different perspectives which shape this and doesn't necessarily mean this will be your experience.
You make your own experience. If you've already defied the odds by getting on to the program, who knows what you could do during it! If you're hyperfocused on a goal and consistently working towards it, SVF could offer an opportunity to help make it happen.
# Should you do SVF?
SVF's value depends on YOU. The alumni that benefit the most are often those who struggled most to get in.
If you're looking for opportunities, are hungry and willing to go the extra mile, then go for it! Plus, it's fun to discover the Bay if you haven't been before! :foggy:
Finally, the SVF team is also doing a great job of building a powerful community that helps each other.
