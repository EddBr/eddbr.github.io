It seems as if AI is actually [unhobbling](https://situational-awareness.ai/from-gpt-4-to-agi/#Unhobbling) humans in work. Software engineers were always capable of writing 10x more code, but handwriting was the bottleneck. This means jobs will persist, right?

Well, some jobs. I think the generalisable rule is that the job must scale.

Software engineers still exist because they can scale. They can be in charge of 10 AI models, coordinate them and produce 10x the output. Engineering roles that didn't scale are redundant, e.g. the junior software engineer.

I suspect this scaling law will hold true for other industries as well. If a lawyer can now write 10x more legal documetnts, they will. Radiologists were predicted to be replaced by AI. Instead, they read more radiographs.

The scale of the work now depends on the scale you can handle. Previously, scaling required managing lots of people, now anyone can manage agents. Inefficiencies and reasonable blockers in the world will prevent it happening everywhere, but I think there is generally room for optimism!

What scale can we handle?
