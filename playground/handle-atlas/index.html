The Handle Atlas

Levers, knobs, knockers, rings, latches and wooden locks from all over the world. Choose one and you are standing at its door, close enough to touch the handle, in 3D. Your pointer becomes a hand: press, turn, pull and lift each handle the way it is really used, and feel how heavy each door is. Scroll to lean in or step back. Turn your sound on.
