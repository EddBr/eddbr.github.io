A working catalogue of light switches

The Light Switch Atlas

Twenty-four ways to turn on a light, from a brass rotary switch screwed to a wooden block to an office sensor that turns the lights off if you sit still too long. Pick one and you are standing at the wall in the dark, in 3D, close enough to touch it. Your pointer becomes a hand: flick, rock, press, turn, pull, touch or clap. Then watch the lamp come on the way that kind of lamp really does: a filament glows up warm, a fluorescent tube stutters, a sodium lamp creeps from red to orange. Scroll to lean in or step back. Turn your sound on.
